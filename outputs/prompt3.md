# Prompt 3

> Prompt 3. Summarize the press conference in bullet points. Highlight every place where the Chair's tone or message diverges from statement.md (more hawkish, more dovish, or adds conditions). Rate each divergence as minor, moderate, or major.

---

Statement paragraphs are cited as **S¶1** (decision), **S¶2** (economy) and **S¶3** (inflation).

## Summary

**Opening statement (pp. 1–4)**
- Restates the statement almost word for word: a ¼-point hike to 3¾–4 percent, solid growth, inflation elevated, a "timelier return" to 2 percent (p. 1).
- Calls the hike a removal of accommodation: broad financial conditions were not restrictive, and the Committee widely shared that view (p. 1).
- Describes the economy as strengthening and the labor market as at full employment ("in good shape"), so the "predominant focus" is on inflation (pp. 1–2).
- Gives inflation numbers (total PCE about 3.6 percent, core PCE about 3.2 percent) and says summer data show no meaningful improvement in the underlying trend. Commodity input prices have risen (p. 2).
- Sets out the Jackson Hole standard for action: confidence that underlying inflation is moving to target "clearly and at sufficient speed". The Committee judged it unmet (pp. 2–3).
- Lists four things policy aims to ensure: credit and financial conditions, no broadening of relative-price changes, low inflation compensation, anchored expectations (p. 3).
- Presents the SEP but has submitted no projection of his own. The median funds rate is 4.1 percent at end-year and next year, and inflation risks are to the upside (p. 3).

**Q&A (pp. 4–15)**
- Refuses forward guidance and won't prejudge future decisions (pp. 4–5, 13).
- Says decisions follow trends, not single data releases. The neutral rate has no operational role (pp. 6, 8).
- Names three reasons for moving since July: a stronger economy, inflation trends failing his test, geopolitics (pp. 6–7).
- Stresses independence ("stay in our lane"). Says no harm to the labor market is needed, and an AI task force will report by year-end (pp. 9, 14).
