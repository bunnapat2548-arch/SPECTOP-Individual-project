# Prompt 1

> Prompt 1. Compare statement.md with transcript.md. List every piece of information in the press conference that was NOT in the statement. For each, give the exact quote, page number, whether it came from the opening statement or Q&A, and why it matters for policy expectations.

---

**Baseline (already in the statement, so excluded):** the ¼-point hike to 3¾–4 percent; ample reserves; activity "expanding at a solid pace"; elevated, partly geopolitical uncertainty; resilient spending; strong productivity; robust capex; job gains keeping pace and unemployment "changed little"; inflation "elevated"; a "timelier return" to 2 percent; "will deliver price stability"; the unanimous (12–0) vote. The first three paragraphs of the opening statement (p. 1) restate these almost word for word.

Direction labels: **H** = hawkish, **D** = dovish, **N** = neutral/procedural.
